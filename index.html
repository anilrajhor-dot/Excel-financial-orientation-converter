Reconstruction

Structure-first: the converter analyses PDF text coordinates before rendering. It does not transpose the underlying matrix to fit a mobile screen. A 20 × 3 financial table remains 20 × 3; the 3 × 20 button creates a display-only transpose. Multi-word particulars stay in one cell and financial numbers are never broken across lines.
